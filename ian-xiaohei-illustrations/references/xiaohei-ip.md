# Xiaohei IP

## Character Definition

Xiaohei is Ian's fixed visual IP for inline article illustrations.

Every image should include Xiaohei by default. Xiaohei is not a mascot, not a sticker, not a cute decoration — he is an absurd worker earnestly participating in the system.

## Appearance

- Solid black small creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- Body can be cylindrical, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, hole, machine interior black block.
- Slightly irregular outline, hand-drawn feel.
- Expression: blank, deadpan, calm, serious.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something a bit absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, not cutesy.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- Like really responsible for some job on a whiteboard sketch.

## Common Roles

Have Xiaohei perform core actions:

- Carrying materials.
- Pulling wires to gather information sources.
- Stuck at a breakpoint.
- Operating a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a sorting funnel.
- Slicing a "material fish".
- Stamping a handoff phrase.
- Leading a handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign watching a pit.
- Reaching out from a hole but not catching content.
- Bricking, bridging, opening doors, sorting, recording nearby.

## Taboos

- Don't draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Don't draw as a children's cartoon character.
- Don't give Xiaohei complex clothing, sticker expressions, or shiny eyes.
- Don't have Xiaohei just standing in the corner watching.
- Don't let Xiaohei steal the structural expression.
- Don't draw Xiaohei too commercial, too rounded, or too refined.

## Judgment Standard

If removing Xiaohei still leaves the core metaphor fully intact, he's too decorative; rewrite the prompt to make Xiaohei the action subject.
