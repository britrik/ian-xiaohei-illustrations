# Style DNA

## In One Sentence

Pure white, minimalist, hand-drawn, white space, restrained, absurd, product-sketch feel, handwritten annotation feel, clear structure but non-instructional.

Like a long-time AI, product, design, or dev tools person, casually sketching an explanation on a blank sheet of paper.

## Must-Haves

- 16:9 landscape inline illustrations.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, vintage paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art as main: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector, not heavy outlines.
- Lots of white space: subject occupies ~40%-60% of the frame, at least 35% blank, ideally one calm empty area.
- Sparse handwritten annotations: at most 5-8 places, each ideally 2-8 characters.
- One image tells only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Structure should express naturally; do not write the structure type name on the image.

## Colors

- Black: main line art, character, frame lines, structure, main text, primary objects.
- Red: key annotations, problems, emotional points, critical reminders, results.
- Orange: main flow, paths, arrows — always flowing LEFT TO RIGHT (Western A→B direction). Never right-to-left.
- Blue: supplementary notes, mental states, system states, second-layer explanations, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required on every image. Colors should be restrained — better too few than too many.

## Absolutely Not

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagrams.
- No refined flat illustration.
- No techy UI.
- No real app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, textures.
- No explaining every node clearly.
- No top-left titles like "Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap".

## Aesthetic Direction

Absurd, creative, interesting, concise and crisp, wildly imaginative.

Not cute, childish, overly complex, or stiff.
